Privacy Policy for WordRush

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the WordRush mobile application ("the App"). The App is built as a simple, standalone competitive word game.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The App operates entirely offline. It does not require a user account, login, or internet connection (after initial download) to function. Your gameplay data, scores, and any other activity are processed locally on your device and are immediately discarded at the end of each session or when the App is closed.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services in this App that collect user data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for children. Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the developer contact email provided in the Play Store listing.
